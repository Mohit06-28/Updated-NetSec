About NetScan

NetScan is a free online network scanner and TCP port scanner. It runs a multi-threaded TCP-connect scan from the cloud and identifies services running on discovered ports.

What works
  • TCP port scanning on public IPs and hostnames
  • Automatic service identification (80+ known ports)
  • Risk scoring for dangerous open ports
  • Scan history, JSON/TXT report export
What does NOT work from the cloud
  • Private LAN scanning (10.x, 172.16-31.x, 192.168.x) — not reachable from the internet
  • ARP / MAC address discovery — requires local network access
  • ICMP ping sweeps — blocked in serverless environments
  • SYN / FIN / NULL / XMAS stealth scans — require raw sockets (not available)
Legal notice: Only scan systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Unauthorized port scanning is illegal in many jurisdictions.
